About Grayson

A love of cars and pride in the finish

Grayson Shainline started Grayson's Automotive Detailing in Pembroke in the summer of 2021. It grew out of a passion for cars and a lot of care for the finish. He runs the business as owner and operator.

He's been voted first in Renfrew County three times: 2023, 2024 and 2026. Every vehicle gets the same meticulous work, whether it's a daily driver, a family SUV or a work truck.
